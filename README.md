# Standard Chartered Credit Analyst Project – Credit Appraisal Model

A credit appraisal model for a corporate loan request, built as a credit analyst practice project. The borrower is **Vardhan Precision Components Pvt. Ltd.** (fictional), an auto-components maker in Pune requesting a **₹20 crore term loan** and renewal of a ₹15 crore cash credit.

## Build
- Three-year income statement and balance sheet (₹ crore)
- Ratio analysis against bank benchmarks: liquidity, leverage, coverage, profitability, working capital
- Loan structure with repayment schedule and five-year projected DSCR
- Security cover, LTV, guarantees and covenants
- **5 Cs of credit** scorecard, internal risk grade and risk-based pricing

## Result
- Recommendation: **APPROVE**, internal grade **CR-2**, weighted score 4.43 / 5
- Minimum projected DSCR **2.39x** (average 3.34x); security cover **1.85x**; LTV 40%
- All 15 ratios pass their benchmarks in FY2026; debt/EBITDA 1.05x, interest cover 7.9x
- Indicative all-in rate **9.75%** (EBLR + 1.0% spread for CR-2)
